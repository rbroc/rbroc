Hi there! 🖖

I am a <span style="color: #A52A2A;">Research Scientist</span> in <span style="color: #A52A2A;">Machine Learning and Cognition</span> in the <span style="color: #A52A2A;">Paradigms of Intelligence team @ Google</span>, where we work on frontier questions concerning the future of artificial, human, and hybrid intelligence. 

Previously, I was an Assistant Professor in Cognition and Computation @ Aarhus University, a postdoc at the Psychoinformatics Lab @ UT Austin, and a Data Fellow @ the United Nations' Centre for Humanitarian Data. I hold an MSc in Cognitive Science from the Centre for Mind/Brain Sciences at the University of Trento, and a PhD in Cognitive Science from Aarhus University.

I do research on a number of questions at the intersection of <span style="color: #A52A2A;">AI and human cognition</span> 🤖 🧠 🗣️. For example, I am interested in <span style="color: #A52A2A;">understanding how AI systems implement high-level cognitive processes</span> such as metacognition, in studying <span style="color: #A52A2A;">emerging forms of collective intelligence</span>, and in understanding the <span style="color: #A52A2A;">cognitive impact of human-AI interaction</span>. With this, I contribute to making AI systems more beneficial for more and more of us.

While I don't actively do much of that these days, I ❤️ contributing to <span style="color: #A52A2A;">open-source software</span>. I contributed to the development of [Neuroscout](https://neuroscout.org/), a platform for end-to-end analysis of neuroimaging data; I implemented deep learning pipelines for the feature extraction library [pliers](https://github.com/PsychoinformaticsLab/pliers); I co-developed [Turftopic](https://github.com/x-tabdeveloping/turftopic), a package providing streamlined implementations of multiple contextualized topic modeling techniques; and I contributed to the [Massive Multilingual Text Embedding Benchmark (MMTEB)](https://github.com/embeddings-benchmark/mteb/blob/main/docs/mmteb/readme.md).

I am also interested in the opportunities opened up by [<span style="color: #A52A2A;">AI in the humanitarian sector</span>](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fdata.2023.1082787/full), and I am always eager to collaborate with researchers and organizations in this space.

You can read more about my work in my [CV](https://rbroc.github.io/cv/cv.pdf) and check out my publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EjB67tAAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). 🚀
